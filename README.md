# See You Tomorrow, Mr. Klein — Interactive Demo

A small, self-contained playable slice of the narrative game. Mr. Klein reviews one household-record discrepancy across two workdays. The office repeats its routine; each decision leaves a human and administrative trace.

## Play

Open index.html in a modern browser. No build step, account, or external assets are required.

1. Begin the workday.
2. Open the file and inspect the registry extract, rent receipt, and neighbor statement.
3. Choose whether to correct the register, uphold the archived record, or attach both accounts. Each choice now opens a form action: strike through and rewrite the status, copy the official entry, or fasten the receipt to the file.
4. Watch the signed copy leave the desk and return to the incoming tray the next morning. The returned document, applicant's need, and evidence vary with the first decision.
5. Review the changed evidence and decide what the second office copy will record.

You can set the form aside before signing. Signing stamps the copy and sends it to the next office. Pen, punch, and stamp sounds accompany the actions; supported phones also give a brief haptic response.

An original Web Audio score adds quiet low tones, sparse notes, rain, and room hum. The sound button in the header mutes both music and effects; the office is fully playable without audio.

The demo has no score or correct answer. It is a prototype of the game's routine, contradictions, and unresolved residue.
